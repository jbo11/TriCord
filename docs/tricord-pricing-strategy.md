# TriCord Subscription Strategy

TriCord now uses one customer-facing subscription model instead of Free, Plus, and Pro tiers.

## Positioning

- Every new Hub starts with a 30-day free trial.
- The trial includes all core product capabilities.
- TriCord does not hide features behind plan tiers during the trial.
- After the trial ends, the Hub becomes read-only until the Owner starts a paid subscription.
- Billing belongs to the Hub, not to the individual user account.
- The Standard Hub is $29/month or $290/year, which gives customers 2 months free on annual billing.
- The Standard Hub includes up to 25 employees, 25 individual mailboxes, 25 GB of storage, unlimited Rooms, unlimited messages, unlimited tasks, unlimited CRM, unlimited recruitment, unlimited knowledge base, and unlimited attendance.
- Shared mailboxes are not included in the 25 mailbox allowance.
- Teams that need more than 25 employees should contact TriCord for a custom plan instead of selecting another public tier.
- Promo codes, taxes, invoices, payment methods, renewal terms, and cancellation are handled through Stripe Checkout and the Stripe Customer Portal.

| Message | Customer Value |

## Billing Behavior

1. A new Hub starts in trial status with trial start and end dates.
2. During trial, all enabled product functionality is available.
3. Before trial expiry, the Owner receives reminders.
4. If the Owner subscribes, the Hub becomes active on the Standard Hub plan.
5. Adding or removing employees within the 25 employee allowance does not create per-seat Stripe charges.
6. If the Hub needs more than 25 employees, the Owner should contact TriCord for a custom plan.
7. If the trial expires without subscription, the Hub becomes read-only.
8. If a subscription is cancelled, the Hub follows cancellation rules configured in Stripe and TriCord access policy.
9. Owners can export Hub data before cancellation or closure.
