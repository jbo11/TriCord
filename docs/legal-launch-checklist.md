# TriCord Legal And Compliance Launch Checklist

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This checklist is not legal advice. It records the legal and compliance guardrails TriCord should satisfy before public paid launch and before collecting unrestricted customer or employee data.

## Business Setup

- Form the operating LLC or corporation before scaling paid subscriptions.
- Open a business bank account and run Stripe, hosting, email, and vendor accounts through the business entity.
- Register the TriCord trade name or DBA if the legal entity name is different.
- Check city, county, state, and federal registration, tax, and business-license requirements.
- Confirm beneficial ownership reporting obligations with counsel or a CPA.

## Public Legal Pages

- Publish Terms and Conditions.
- Publish Privacy Policy.
- Publish Acceptable Use Policy.
- Publish Refund and Cancellation Policy.
- Publish Subprocessor List.
- Publish Security Overview.
- Publish Accessibility Statement.
- Have counsel review all policies before relying on them as final customer contracts.

## Product Positioning

- Describe TriCord as a work hub and operational recordkeeping tool.
- Use “payroll preparation” instead of “payroll processing” unless a licensed/payroll-compliant workflow is implemented.
- Do not claim HIPAA compliance, legal compliance, tax compliance, payroll compliance, or guaranteed security.
- Do not use fake testimonials, fake customer logos, or unapproved customer names.

## Regulated Data

- Prohibit protected health information and medical records until a HIPAA program, BAA, audit trail, breach process, and security controls are intentionally implemented.
- Prohibit full payment card numbers, bank login credentials, biometric identifiers, classified data, and government secrets.
- Require Hub Owners to provide employee notices before collecting HR, payroll-preparation, GPS, IP, device, or selfie data.
- Avoid facial recognition or biometric verification until legal review is complete.

## Privacy And Security Operations

- Maintain a current subprocessor list.
- Keep production and development data separate.
- Rotate leaked or exposed secrets immediately.
- Use least-privilege service keys and role-based app permissions.
- Test tenant isolation and role access before every production release.
- Maintain incident response, data export, deletion request, and breach-notification procedures.
- Add analytics or advertising scripts only after updating privacy notices and consent controls.

## Billing And Email

- Confirm Stripe product names, prices, billable-seat behavior, promo-code behavior, taxes, proration, cancellation, and refund rules match public pricing copy.
- Keep outgoing email limited to authorized business communications.
- Prevent use of TriCord email features for spam, phishing, deceptive messages, or unlawful marketing.
- Keep inbound email warnings clear: forwarded messages may contain confidential data and should not include prohibited regulated data.
